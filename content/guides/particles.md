## Max Particles and Power Spots

Max Particles, or **MP**, are used to enter Max Battles and train Max Moves. Collect them at Power Spots and while exploring.

Under normal conditions:

- You can collect up to **800 MP per day**.
- You can carry up to **1,500 MP**.
- Your first interaction with a Power Spot can award extra particles.
- Special events may temporarily raise these limits.

Plan your collection before Max Monday or a community battle session. Max Monday runs from **6:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. local time**, so starting with plenty of MP lets you battle, collect more and repeat.

## Leaving a Pokémon at a Power Spot

After a win, you can station one of your battle-party Pokémon at the Power Spot. It helps later Trainers through an attack bonus and can earn Candy before returning to you.

Recall it when you need it or when the Power Spot closes. Do not leave a Pokémon you expect to use immediately in another battle.

## Particle planning

Before a planned session:

1. Check how much MP you already hold.
2. Check the entry cost of the planned boss.
3. Leave room to collect more MP during the session.
4. Avoid spending large amounts on Max Move upgrades immediately before the event unless the upgrade is needed for that battle.

Max Particles are deducted only after a win, so a failed attempt can be retried without paying the entry cost again.
