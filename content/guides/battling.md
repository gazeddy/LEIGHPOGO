## Battling

Max Battles alternate between a normal phase and a Max phase. The aim is to fill the Max Meter, survive the boss's attacks and use the three Max turns effectively.

## Normal phase

The aim is to fill the Max Meter without losing the team.

- Keep attacking to build the meter.
- Short Fast Attacks are often useful because they can be repeated quickly.
- Use Charged Attacks when they improve the matchup rather than pressing them automatically.
- Swipe into the energy bubbles on the battlefield for extra Max Meter progress.
- Dodge when it will keep an important tank, healer or attacker alive.

There is no normal raid-style countdown, but the boss eventually becomes desperate and starts dealing enormous damage. Do not treat the battle as unlimited time.

## Max phase

Each Trainer receives three Max turns:

- **Attacker:** use the attacking Max Move.
- **Tank:** use Max Guard.
- **Healer:** use Max Spirit when healing is needed; otherwise attack.

Coordinate before difficult battles. Four people all assuming somebody else brought healing usually ends badly.

## Fainting and retrying

If all three Pokémon faint in a three-star battle or higher, keep using **Cheer**. Your Cheers help the remaining Trainers fill their Max Meters.

In a larger Gigantamax battle, cheering remains useful for as long as other Trainers are still fighting. A failed attempt does not consume the entry particles, so regroup, adjust the teams and retry before the Power Spot closes.
