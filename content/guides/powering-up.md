## Powering up a Max Pokémon

Max Pokémon are expensive because normal levels and Max Moves are upgraded separately. Fully maxing everything is not necessary.

Choose a useful species with good attacks and defensive typing. Better IVs are welcome, but the correct species, moves and role matter much more than the small difference between good and perfect IVs.

## Recommended upgrade order

1. **Evolve it to its final useful form.**
2. **Power it to around level 40.** Level 50 helps in the hardest battles but is not the first priority.
3. **Train its attacking Max Move.**
4. **Unlock Max Guard or Max Spirit when it has a support role.**
5. **Raise moves further or go to level 50 only when the battle or long-term value justifies the cost.**

Candy, Candy XL and MP costs vary by species. Legendary and higher-tier builds can consume hundreds of Candy and Candy XL plus thousands of MP, so check the complete cost before committing.

> **Trading warning:** trading a Max Pokémon resets its trained Max Move levels. Trade first, then invest.

## Spend for a purpose

Before upgrading, decide what the Pokémon is being built to do:

- An attacker mainly needs the correct Fast Attack, useful offensive typing and a trained attacking Max Move.
- A tank needs bulk, resistance to the boss and Max Guard.
- A healer needs enough bulk to survive and Max Spirit.

A useful level-40 Pokémon with the right move trained is usually better value than an unfinished level-50 project that cannot perform its intended role.
