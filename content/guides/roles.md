## Team roles

Difficult Max Battles are easier when the group agrees roles before entering.

## Attacker

The cheapest initial role. Concentrate on:

- A Fast Attack that produces the Max Move type required for the boss.
- Super-effective damage.
- Training the attacking Max Move.

During the Max phase, attack unless the group urgently needs support.

## Tank

A tank needs bulk, resistance to the boss's attacks and a trained **Max Guard**. Use Guard during the Max phase to reduce incoming damage and keep the team alive for another cycle.

A good tank does not have to deal the most damage. Its job is to survive, protect the team and buy time for the attackers.

## Healer

A healer needs enough bulk to survive and a trained **Max Spirit**. During the Max phase, check the team health bars: heal when needed and attack when everybody is healthy.

Do not wait until the whole team is nearly fainted before healing. Keeping important Pokémon alive is usually more valuable than squeezing in one extra attack.

## Suggested team of four

A strong starting arrangement is:

- Two attackers
- One tank
- One healer

Training all three Max Moves gives flexibility, but it is expensive. Build the role the group needs first and coordinate before entering the battle.
