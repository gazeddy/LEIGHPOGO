## Before following a counter guide

Check when the guide was written and whether it covers the current boss, moveset and battle mechanics. Max Battle recommendations can change when new Max Pokémon become available.

Use external recommendations as a starting point, then compare them with:

- The Max Pokémon you actually own.
- The Candy, Candy XL and Max Particles you can afford to spend.
- The roles already covered by the rest of the group.
- The boss's possible attacks and defensive typing.

The most expensive recommendation is not automatically the best choice for your group.
